About Us

Designing a sustainable society with culture-based technology.

COCI PLUS Co., Ltd. (COCI+) builds a Culture Technology (CT)-based creative platform and researches a wide range of technologies.

Centered on developing CT-based creative platforms, ESG-oriented products, and patent filings for intellectual property, we support the growth environment of companies and creators with AI and XR technologies.
